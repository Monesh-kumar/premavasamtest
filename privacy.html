3. Lawful Basis & Purpose

Why We Process Your Data & Legal Grounds

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act 2023) and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, we process your personal data on the following lawful grounds:

  • Consent: Given freely and explicitly by you when you submit a contribution form, volunteer application, meal booking, or contact inquiry. You may withdraw consent at any time, subject to statutory retention obligations.
  • Statutory Tax Obligation (Section 80G & Form 10BD): Under Section 80G(5)(viii) and Section 139A of the Indian Income Tax Act, 1961 read with Rule 18AB of the Income Tax Rules, registered charitable trusts are legally required to collect donor identification (specifically PAN for domestic contributions) and submit an annual electronic Statement of Donations in Form 10BD to the Income Tax Department. This reporting is mandatory to enable donors to claim Section 80G tax deductions on their income tax returns.
  • Legitimate Operational Needs: Coordinating meal distribution for our resident children, verifying bank credits, issuing formal 80G tax certificates, managing volunteer programs, and ensuring digital platform security.
4. Data Protection & Security

Security Measures & Technical Safeguards

We implement comprehensive technical, organizational, and physical security measures to safeguard your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure:

  • Encryption in Transit: All web traffic is protected with TLS/HTTPS, ensuring data submitted through our forms cannot be intercepted in transit.
  • Database Hardening: Application data is stored in an encrypted/restricted SQLite database with Write-Ahead Logging (WAL) and strict file-system permissions accessible only by the application service.
  • Access Control: Administrative access is strictly limited to authorized trust personnel using strong, rotating authentication credentials with brute-force rate-limiting defenses.
  • Content Security & XSS Prevention: All user-supplied data displayed in management dashboards is rendered using safe DOM text manipulation, preventing Cross-Site Scripting (XSS). Strict Content Security Policy (CSP), anti-framing, and MIME-type security headers are enforced across all pages.
  • Automated Backups: Secure, WAL-safe database backups are maintained to prevent data loss.
5. Data Disclosures

Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosures

Prema Vasam will never sell, rent, commercialize, or share your personal data with marketing agencies or third parties.

  • Technical Infrastructure Service Providers: Trusted enterprise service providers (such as hosting and network infrastructure providers) who operate under strict non-disclosure obligations and process data solely to maintain website availability.
  • Legal Compulsion: If required by a lawful court order, subpoena, or government regulatory directive.
6. Data Retention

Data Retention & Disposal

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or to satisfy legal, accounting, and tax requirements:

  • Donation & 80G Tax Records: Retained for a minimum of 7 to 10 financial years as required by Indian income tax and trust audit statutes.
  • Volunteer & Inquiry Records: Retained for up to 3 years to facilitate ongoing volunteer engagement and correspondence, after which records are reviewed and securely purged, or erased earlier upon verified request.
  • Server Security Logs: Retained for up to 90 days for forensic monitoring and DDoS defense before automated rotation.
7. Your Rights

Your Rights under the DPDP Act 2023

As a Data Principal under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Information & Summary: You may request a summary of personal data held about you and the processing activities undertaken.
  • Right to Correction & Updation: You may request correction of inaccurate data or completion of incomplete information.
  • Right to Erasure: You may request deletion of your personal data, provided its retention is not legally required under Indian tax or charitable trust accounting laws.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: You have the right to register a grievance with our designated Grievance Officer regarding any data processing concern.
  • Right to Nominate: You have the right to nominate another individual who, in the event of death or incapacity, may exercise these rights on your behalf.
