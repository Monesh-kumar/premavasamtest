Our Origin Story

How Love Rewrote the Destiny of Special Children

In 1999, clinical psychologist Mr. Selvyn Roy met Indra, a 14-year-old girl battling deep depression because societal prejudice barred her from school due to her disability.

"Under loving guidance, Indra scored 420/500 in her SSLC exams, graduated with first-class honors, completed a Master's degree from prestigious Anna University, and now directs Prem Illam."

That same month, an abandoned boy was rescued in critical condition from Chennai's streets. Named Prem, his home became Prema Vasam. Today, coordinator Miss Mumtaj and our dedicated multi-rehabilitation team ensure this circle of love reaches over 200 children.
