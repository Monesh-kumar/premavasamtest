Our Origin Story

How Love Rewrote the Destiny of Special Children

In 1999, clinical psychologist Mr. Selvyn Roy met Miss D.Indra, a 14-year-old girl battling deep depression because societal prejudice barred her from school due to her disability.

"Under loving guidance, Miss D.Indra scored 420/500 in her SSLC exams, graduated with first-class honors, completed a Master's degree (MCA) from prestigious Anna University, and now directs Prem Illam."

That same month, a young boy was rescued in critical condition from Chennai's streets. Named Prem, his home became Prema Vasam. Today, coordinator Miss Mumtaj and our dedicated multi-rehabilitation team ensure this circle of love reaches over 200 children.
