How to Reach Us

✈️ From Chennai Airport (MAA):

Approx. 12 km (25 mins drive). Take Pallavaram–Thoraipakkam 200 Feet Radial Rd → Kundrathur Main Rd towards Gerugambakkam.

🚆 From Chennai Central / Egmore:

Take suburban train / metro to Guindy. From Guindy, direct buses (54, 54B, 88M) connect to Porur / Kovur stop.

🚌 From Koyambedu CMBT:

Buses towards Kundrathur / Kovur via Porur Tollgate. Autos and app cabs (Uber/Ola) are easily available.
